## 3. Location

Location is read only while the app is open and only for the purposes above. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in your device settings; the features that need it will then not work. Drivers stop sharing their location by going offline.

## 4. Who receives data

- The other party of a job sees your name, phone number, truck details (drivers) and live position as needed for that job.
- Our administrators see driver documents and reports to verify drivers and resolve disputes.
- Processors acting for us: Google (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging for sign-in and notifications), Mapbox (maps, routes and address search, which receive the coordinates or addresses you look up), [HOSTING PROVIDER] (servers and database), and Sentry (error reports, if and when enabled; it receives technical data about crashes and errors).
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these processors are located outside Tunisia. [CONFIRM TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS AND INPDP AUTHORISATION.]
